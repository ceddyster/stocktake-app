WHAT THE SHEET DID, AND WHAT THE APP DOES
Sorting and finding

Category and sub-category order is kept exactly as the workbook has it. Sub-category headers are sticky and collapsible, the chips filter the list down to one sub-category, and the search box never scrolls away.

Cartons and singles

Two fields, carton size from column E, total units done for you. Spirits and postmix accept a decimal for the part bottle.

Two people, one location

Nobody gets locked out. If a line gets counted twice it is flagged on the row and resolved from the home screen.
