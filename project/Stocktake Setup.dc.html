Each phone appends only its own lines, so two people counting at once never overwrite each other. The app reads the file back and takes the latest entry per product per place — and when the same line has two names against it, both are kept until someone picks. HOW SETUP WORKS
Your own places

Bars, venues, fridges, storerooms, vans — call them whatever you call them. Rename or remove one later and the counts follow.

One product list

Every place counts the same list, so nothing gets missed. Build it product by product, or attach an .xlsx or .csv — a column of product names is enough, and it picks up barcodes, categories and carton sizes if they are there.

Three to five counters

Everyone types their name, joins on a five-character code, and every line carries who entered it. Count the same line twice and both numbers are kept side by side until the admin picks. Everyone's counts land in the same shared stocktake within a second or so.

OPEN

Every count saves on the phone as well as to the shared stocktake, so closing the app or losing signal keeps your place. Unit costs and par levels are not in here yet.
